Verifikasi sudut benturan (joint) antara dua batang baja penyangga rangka atap baja.

Vektor Batang:

\( \vec{u} = 2\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} + 1\mathbf{k} \)

\( \vec{v} = 0\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k} \)

1. Dot Product:

\( \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = (2)(0) + (2)(4) + (1)(3) \)

\( \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0 + 8 + 3 = 11 \)

2. Panjang Vektor:

\( |\vec{u}| = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 + 1^2} = 3 \text{ m} \)

\( |\vec{v}| = \sqrt{0^2 + 4^2 + 3^2} = 5 \text{ m} \)

3. Sudut Jepit (\( \theta \)):

\( \cos(\theta) = \frac{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}}{|\vec{u}| |\vec{v}|} \)

\( \cos(\theta) = \frac{11}{3 \times 5} = \frac{11}{15} \)

\( \theta = \arccos\left(\frac{11}{15}\right) \approx 42.83^\circ \)
